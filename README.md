## 🚀 What I'm building

### [OPREZO](https://oprezo.co)
*Defterden kurtulun. İşletmenizi tek ekrandan yönetin.*

A business management platform for anyone who rents things by the hour or the day: football fields, boat tours, photo studios, salons... basically everyone still running their bookings from a paper notebook. Reservations, customers and payments, all in one panel.

- 🌐 **[oprezo.co](https://oprezo.co)**: the website
- 📅 **[app.oprezo.co](https://app.oprezo.co)**: the business panel with calendar, bookings and payments
- 🛠️ A separate admin panel for running the platform
- 📱 **[iOS app](https://apps.apple.com/tr/app/oprezo/id6816398962)** live on the App Store, Android in closed testing
- ✉️ Curious about Oprezo? [merhaba@oprezo.co](mailto:merhaba@oprezo.co)
